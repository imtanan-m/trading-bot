        Ye app kya hai?

        7 trading bots GitHub ke cloud pe har ~15 minute mein chalte hain (in mein 2 agents hain jo Binance ke top 100 coins scan karte hain), chahe aap ka PC band ho. Sab paper trading karte hain: har bot ke paas $1000 nakli paisa hai, lekin prices Binance ki asli live prices hain. Yahan aap dekh sakte hain kaun si strategy asal mein kaam karti hai, bina asli paisa lagaye.

        Bot trade kyun nahi kar raha?

        Zyada tar bots sirf uptrend mein khareedte hain. Market neeche ho to wo cash mein rehte hain, aur yahi unki hifazat hai. Market tab mein "Bot radar" dekhein: wahan dikhta hai ke har bot ki kitni sharten poori hain.

        Telegram pe alerts kaise on karein?
        1. Telegram mein @BotFather ko /newbot bhejein, naam dein, aur jo token mile wo copy karein.
        2. Apne naye bot ko koi bhi message bhejein. Phir @userinfobot se apna chat id lein.
        3. GitHub repo → Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → New repository secret: TELEGRAM_TOKEN aur TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID.
        4. Bas. Har khareed/farokht pe phone pe message aayega.
        Settings ya naya bot?

        Claude ko batayein ke kya chahiye (naya coin, nayi strategy, risk kam/zyada). Wo code aur ye app dono update kar dega.

        Alfaaz ka matlab
        Return
        Shuru ke $1000 se ab tak kitna faida ya nuqsan.
        Max giraav
        Sab se oonchi value se sab se bari girawat (drawdown). Ye risk ka asli paimana hai.
        Stop-loss
        Wo price jahan bot nuqsan rokne ke liye khud bech deta hai.
        Win rate
        Kitni trades faide mein band huin. Trend bots ka win rate kam hota hai, lekin jeet bari hoti hai.
        Profit factor
        Kul faida ÷ kul nuqsan. 1 se upar matlab faida.
        Backtest
        Strategy ko purane data pe chala ke dekhna. Future ki guarantee nahi.
        Kill switch
        Bot bohot zyada gir jaye to sab bech ke khud band ho jata hai.
        Candle
        Ek waqt ka hissa (1 ghanta, 4 ghante, 1 din). Bot har band candle pe faisla karta hai.

        Ye financial advice nahi hai. Paper trading ke results asli trading se behtar ho sakte hain (asli mein slippage aur jazbaat dono hote hain).
